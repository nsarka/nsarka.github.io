## Biography

[Nick Sarkauskas](https://linkedin.com/in/nsarka/) (nsarka) is a software engineer at NVIDIA working on communication libraries such as [UCC](https://github.com/openucx/ucc), [NCCL](https://github.com/nvidia/nccl), and closed source NCCL plugins (net and profiler) for NVIDIA Spectrum-X deployments. His research interests include a variety of systems, networking, and AI topics.

Previously, he was concurrently an [M.S. student](https://nowlab.cse.ohio-state.edu/people/) at The Ohio State University studying HPC under advisor D.K. Panda, and a part-time MPI software engineer at [X-ScaleSolutions](https://x-scalesolutions.com/), a Columbus-based startup specializing in a range of high-performance and scalable solutions for HPC systems.
